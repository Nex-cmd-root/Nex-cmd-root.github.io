Essential Indoor Care Fundamentals

💧

The 2-Inch Moisture Test

Overwatering is the #1 cause of plant loss. Check topsoil with your finger—water only when dry to the touch.

  • Warm months: Every 7-10 days
  • Cool Ndola winter: Reduce by 50%
☀️

Bright, Filtered Light

Tropical plants love bright natural daylight but should be shielded from harsh direct midday scorch.

  • East windows: Gentle morning sun
  • Low light lovers: Snake plants & ZZ

Core Fundamentals

The Three Golden Rules of Thriving Greenery

💧

1. The 2-Inch Moisture Rule

Overwatering kills 90% of indoor plants. Insert your finger 2 inches into the soil. If it feels cool and damp, do not water. If dry to the touch, water slowly until moisture trickles through drainage holes.

  • Warm months: Check every 5–7 days
  • Cool winter: Check every 12–14 days
☀️

2. Bright, Indirect Light is King

Most tropical houseplants naturally grow under rainforest canopies. They love bright, filtered natural daylight but will scorch if placed under harsh midday direct sunbeams.

  • East-facing windows: Gentle morning sun
  • South/West windows: Diffuse with sheer curtains
🪴

3. Drainage is Non-Negotiable

Roots require oxygen just like leaves. Never let potted plants sit in stagnant standing saucer water for more than 20 minutes after watering. Always empty drainage trays.

  • Use pots with drainage holes
  • Add 20% perlite or pumice to potting soil
Climate Adaptation

Seasonal Plant Care Across Zambia (Ndola Climate)

How to adjust watering, feeding, and light throughout the Copperbelt and Zambian weather cycles.

🌧️ Nov – April

The Rainy & Warm Season

High Humidity & Active Growth: Plants experience rapid new leaf growth. However, ambient air moisture means soil dries out slower.

✅ Action: Reduce watering frequency by 20%

✅ Action: Fertilize with organic plant food once a month

⚠️ Watch out: Inspect for fungus gnats in damp topsoil

❄️ May – August

The Cool & Dry Season

Dormancy & Cooler Nights: Growth slows down significantly as temperatures drop during winter nights in Ndola.

✅ Action: Cut watering in half (water every 14-20 days)

✅ Action: Stop all chemical fertilizer feeding

⚠️ Watch out: Keep plants away from cold window drafts

🔥 Sept – October

The Hot & Dry Season

Peak Evaporation & Heat Stress: High midday temperatures and dry winds cause rapid soil dehydration.

✅ Action: Check soil every 3-4 days; water in the morning

✅ Action: Mist large-leaf plants (Anthuriums/Monstera) daily

⚠️ Watch out: Pull delicate foliage back from hot window glass
